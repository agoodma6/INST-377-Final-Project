How To Use Historic UMD Weather Planner

  1. Go to the Home page.
  2. Use the UMD latitude and longitude already filled in, or enter another location.
  3. Select a past date.
  4. Click Search Weather.
  5. Look at the temperature, chart, and map marker.
  6. Click Save Weather Day if you want to save the result.
  7. Go to Saved Days to view saved weather records.
